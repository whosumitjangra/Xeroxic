Find Your Email

For Army Institute of Technology (AIT) students, your login email is your registered college email (e.g. rollnumber@aitpune.edu.in).

If you signed up with a personal Gmail account, check your registration inbox or visit the campus Xerox operator counter.

Password Recovery

To reset your student account password, please visit the Xerox Centre desk or ask the Super Admin to issue an instant password reset.

Once reset, you will receive temporary credentials to sign in immediately.
